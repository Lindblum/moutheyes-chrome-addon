# Moutheyes privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-10-23*

Moutheyes does all of its work inside your browser. It does not collect, send, sell or share any
personal data or browsing data, and it has no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.

## What it reads

- **Videos on the pages you visit.** Frames are read locally to find faces and draw the effect.
  They are never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Pictures on the pages you visit**, only if you tick *Images* in the popup. To read a picture
  from another site, the extension downloads it itself, without your cookies. The download goes to
  the site the picture comes from, just as when the page loads it.

## What it stores, on your computer only

- **Your settings** (the popup's switches and sliders), in the extension's local storage.
- **An image cache**: for pictures it has looked at, the picture's address, where faces were found,
  and the moutheyes versions it made. This lets pictures you have seen before show up instantly. It
  is limited to 256 MB, older entries are removed first, and the popup's *Clear* button deletes it.
  It never leaves your computer.

## Third parties

None. The face-tracking model (Google MediaPipe) is bundled with the extension and runs offline.
MediaPipe's built-in usage logging is switched off in this extension.
